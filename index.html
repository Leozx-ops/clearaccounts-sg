FAQ
Common questions
Do I need to be based in Singapore?
No. We work with Singapore-registered companies remotely. All document sharing is done securely via Google Drive.
What accounting software do you use?
We primarily use Xero, which is IRAS-compliant and easy to review anytime. We can also work with QuickBooks or your existing setup.
Is GST filing included in all plans?
GST filing is included in the Growth and Complete plans. Starter clients can add GST filing for SGD 80 per quarter.
How do I send you my documents?
We set up a shared Google Drive folder for your business. Upload your bank statements, invoices, and receipts — we handle everything from there.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any month end with no penalties or lock-in fees.
Are you a licensed public accountant?
We provide bookkeeping and tax preparation services based on professional experience. We are not a licensed public accountant firm. For statutory audit requirements, we will advise accordingly.
